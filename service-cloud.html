FAQ

Cloud & DevOps: questions clients ask first

Not answered here? Ask us directly, we reply within one business day.

Which cloud provider do you recommend?

It depends on your stack and team. .NET workloads often fit Azure well, Node and Next.js products often run cheaply on Vercel or AWS with Cloudflare in front. We give a cost comparison for your case.

Can you reduce our cloud costs?

Usually yes. Idle resources, oversized instances and missing caching are common. A cost review typically finds 20 to 40 percent savings without reducing performance.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Managed operations plans include monitoring, patching, backups, monthly reviews and incident response with defined response times.

Will our developers be able to deploy themselves?

That is the goal. Pipelines are documented and simple: merge to main, tests run, deploy happens. We train your team during handover.
