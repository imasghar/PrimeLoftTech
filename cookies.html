Cookie Policy

This policy explains how PrimeLoft Tech uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help us recognize your browser and remember your preferences.

2. Cookie Categories

  • Essential Cookies: Always enabled. Required for fundamental features like page navigation, theme preference, and security.
  • Analytics Cookies: Optional. Measures traffic sources, page views, and performance to help us improve site user experience.
  • Marketing & Social Cookies: Optional. Used to track visitors across websites and deliver targeted communications via platforms like Meta and LinkedIn.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time by clicking Manage Cookie Preferences in our website footer.
