Cookie Policy

This policy explains how PrimeLoft Tech uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website, and how you can control them.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognise your browser and remember your preferences, such as your light or dark theme choice.

2. Cookie categories

  • Essential: always on. Required for navigation, security, theme preference and remembering your consent choice.
  • Analytics and performance: optional. Measure traffic sources, page views and performance so we can improve the site.
  • Marketing and social: optional. Used by platforms such as Meta and LinkedIn to measure campaigns and show relevant content.

3. Managing your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking Cookie preferences in the website footer. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Contact

Questions about cookies? Email contact@primeloft.tech.
