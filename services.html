Everything you need to know about our services, delivery timelines, engineering standards, and post-launch support.

We provide website and custom software development, mobile apps, UI/UX design, SEO, AI and automation, cloud and DevOps, testing, maintenance, WordPress and Squarespace development. Use the service links above to explore the scope of each offering.

We provide two flexible engagement models: Fixed-Price Milestones for defined project scopes with concrete deliverables, and Dedicated Monthly Retainers for fast-moving teams that require ongoing engineering and design capacity. You receive clear scope breakdowns and transparent cost estimates upfront.

Timelines depend on the deliverables, integrations, content and review stages. We discuss those requirements before providing an estimate and agree milestones for the scope, rather than applying one timeline to every website, app or software project.

Ownership and handover of the agreed code and design deliverables are set out in the proposal, including milestone settlement and account access. Third-party software, fonts, plugins and services remain subject to their own licenses.

We build with speed and search discoverability in mind. Our work can include semantic HTML5, clean structured data (JSON-LD), on-page keyword architecture, technical SEO auditing, Core Web Vitals optimization, and targeted off-page link building strategies.

Yes. Post-launch support for bug fixes and performance tuning can be included in your project scope, and we offer ongoing maintenance arrangements covering security updates, infrastructure monitoring, backups and feature iterations, agreed case by case.
