What is WordPress?

WordPress is an open-source content management system (CMS) used to create and manage websites. It separates everyday publishing from development, so your team can update pages, publish articles and organize content through an editor.

PrimeLoft shapes the design, content structure and editing experience around your business. That can mean a service website, a growing publication, a WooCommerce store or a rebuild of a site you have outgrown.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Your WordPress questions, answered.

Can we edit the website ourselves?

Yes. We plan editable sections and reusable blocks around your content, then walk your team through common publishing tasks. Structural changes or new functionality may still need development.

Do you build custom themes or use existing themes?

The choice depends on your budget, design needs and existing setup. We can develop a custom theme or adapt a suitable existing foundation, explaining the tradeoffs before work starts.

Can you move our existing website to WordPress?

Yes. We first review the source platform, content, URLs and integrations. The proposal identifies what can be transferred, what needs rebuilding and the redirects and launch checks required.

Are hosting and plugin licenses included?

Hosting, domains and paid plugin licenses are identified separately in the proposal. Your business should own its accounts; we can help select and configure the agreed services.

How much does a WordPress website cost?

Cost depends on page count, design complexity, content migration, commerce and integrations. Share your current site and requirements so we can propose a defined scope and estimate.

Can you improve an existing WordPress site?

Yes. We can review its design, editing experience, performance and technical condition, then prioritize a focused improvement project or a larger rebuild.

Do you develop WooCommerce websites?

Yes. We can scope product catalogs, store layouts, cart and checkout configuration, with payment, shipping, tax or inventory integrations selected for your business requirements and regional availability. Hosting, licenses and third-party services are identified separately.

Can you fix a WordPress website built by another developer?

We can begin with a review of the theme, plugins, editing setup and reported issues. Suitable access, backups and an agreed scope are needed before making changes. Findings may lead to focused fixes, customization or a separately scoped redesign.

Can you improve WordPress website speed?

We can investigate image delivery, caching, mobile loading and theme, plugin or script overhead, then prioritize changes within the agreed scope. Results depend on the site, hosting and third-party services; no fixed performance score is promised.

Do you provide WordPress maintenance after launch?

Updates, compatibility checks, backups, bug fixes and small improvements can be covered through a separate maintenance arrangement. We agree the work allowance, reporting route and support expectations; hosting and paid licenses are not automatically included.
