What is mobile app development?

Mobile app development is the process of planning, designing, building and maintaining applications for phones and tablets. It includes the interface people use and any backend services needed for accounts, content or other features.

Native apps are built for a particular platform, such as iOS or Android. Cross-platform development shares parts of the code across platforms. The right approach depends on your audience, device features and maintenance needs.

CHOOSE THE RIGHT FOUNDATION

Which platform fits?

Your audience, device features and maintenance needs should guide the technology choice.

iOS app development

Built for the Apple experience.

Native iPhone and iPad applications using Swift, with layouts and interactions planned for the devices your audience uses. We scope device capabilities, account flows and backend connections around your product.

WHEN TO CONSIDER IT

A focused Apple product or a feature that needs close integration with iOS.

Android app development

Ready for a varied device landscape.

Native Android applications using Kotlin, with attention to screen sizes, navigation and the devices included in your testing plan. The scope can cover camera workflows, notifications and connections to existing services.

WHEN TO CONSIDER IT

Android-first audiences, business devices or platform-specific functionality.

Cross-platform development

A shared foundation for both platforms.

Flutter and React Native can share substantial application code across iOS and Android. We assess the required integrations and platform-specific work before choosing an approach, then test each platform separately.

WHEN TO CONSIDER IT

Products that need both platforms and can benefit from a shared codebase.

PLANNING YOUR APP

Questions worth asking.

How much does mobile app development cost?

The cost depends on the user journeys, platforms, integrations, backend requirements and testing scope. We review your idea and define the first release before providing an estimate. A focused MVP and a complex multi-role application require different budgets.

Should I choose native, Flutter or React Native?

The right choice depends on device features, existing technology, performance requirements and ongoing maintenance. Native Swift and Kotlin offer platform-specific development; Flutter and React Native can share code across platforms. We discuss the tradeoffs for your particular app.

Can you develop an MVP for a startup?

Yes. We help identify the core problem and prioritize the features needed for a useful first release. Prototyping and staged development give you opportunities to review the idea before expanding the scope.

How long does it take to build an app?

The timeline depends on the design, features, integrations and feedback cycles. We agree milestones after discovery and separate development work from any external app review or service setup dependencies.

Do you help with App Store and Google Play submission?

We can prepare builds and listing materials and support submission through your developer accounts. Store approval and review timing are controlled by the platform operators, and any requested changes are reviewed against the agreed scope.

Can you improve an existing mobile app?

Yes. We can begin with a review of the interface, codebase and technical constraints. Depending on the findings, the work may cover usability improvements, integrations, bug fixes or a staged modernization.

Will I own the app and source code?

Ownership, source files and account access are set out in the project agreement. We plan handoff and releases through your accounts, with any third-party licenses and ongoing service costs identified separately.

Do you provide mobile app maintenance?

Maintenance can include bug fixes, OS compatibility work and prioritized improvements. We agree the support coverage and schedule separately so you know what is included after release.

What would make your app worth opening?

Share the idea, the audience and the problem you want to solve. We will help define a focused first release.
