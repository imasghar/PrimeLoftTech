FAQ

QA & Testing: questions clients ask first

Not answered here? Ask us directly, we reply within one business day.

Can you test a product you did not build?

Yes. Independent QA is one of our most common engagements. We onboard with your documentation and a short walkthrough, then start with exploratory testing of critical flows.

How much automation is enough?

Enough to cover the journeys that would hurt most if broken, such as sign-up, checkout and core workflows. We avoid automating everything because brittle suites slow teams down.

Do you test mobile apps on real devices?

Yes, on physical iOS and Android devices as well as cloud device farms for broader coverage.

How do you report bugs?

In your tracker (Jira, Linear, GitHub) with steps to reproduce, expected versus actual results, screenshots or videos, environment details and a severity rating.
