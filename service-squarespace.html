What is Squarespace?

Squarespace is a hosted website platform that brings website building and content management into one service. Businesses use it to present their work, publish content and sell online without managing a separate hosting stack.

Our work brings your brand, page structure and customer journey together within that platform. We help you choose the right approach, customize the experience and learn how to keep your site current.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Your Squarespace questions, answered.

Can Squarespace look different from a template?

Yes. Layout, typography, imagery and content structure can give your site a distinctive character. Where useful, we scope custom CSS or supported enhancements while keeping the editing experience manageable.

Is Squarespace right for my business?

It can suit portfolios, service businesses and stores with requirements supported by the platform. We review complex workflows, integration needs and commerce requirements before recommending it over WordPress or custom development.

Will I be able to update my website?

Yes. We organize the site so you can manage routine text, images and content, and provide a walkthrough at handoff. More advanced layout changes may need additional design support.

Is a Squarespace subscription included?

The platform subscription, domain and any paid scheduling, marketing or third-party services are separate unless explicitly included in your proposal. We help identify the services your site needs.

Can you transfer my existing website?

We review what the current platform can export and what Squarespace can import. Some content and functionality may need manual rebuilding. We agree the migration scope and URL plan before starting.

Do you provide content and ongoing updates?

Content structure, copy support and ongoing updates can be included in the agreed scope. We confirm who supplies photography, brand assets and final copy before the project begins.

Will redesigning my Squarespace website affect SEO?

A redesign can change URLs, content and how pages are linked. We review important pages, retain useful URLs and metadata where possible, plan redirects and check links, sitemap and available Search Console evidence after launch. These checks reduce avoidable mistakes but cannot guarantee unchanged rankings.

Can you build a Squarespace online store?

Yes. We can scope product catalogs, storefront and product layouts, available checkout settings and supported payment options. Feasibility depends on your requirements, region and platform plan. Subscriptions, transaction costs and third-party services are identified separately.

Can you fix a Squarespace website built by someone else?

We can review the existing layouts, content, settings and integrations, then agree focused changes or a broader redesign. The work depends on account access, customizations and platform limits. No unrestricted server-side access is assumed.

Can you improve Squarespace website SEO?

We can review metadata, headings, image alt text, internal links, URL structure and mobile usability, with Search Console checks where access is available. Broader keyword, content and technical SEO work is scoped separately. Rankings and traffic outcomes are not guaranteed.

How is a Squarespace project priced?

Pricing depends on page count, design and content needs, commerce, integrations, custom styling and migration work. We review your existing site or brief before estimating a defined scope. Platform subscriptions, domains and paid services are identified separately.
