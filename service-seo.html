What is SEO, and how does it help your website?

SEO stands for search engine optimization. It helps search engines discover and understand your pages, while helping people decide whether those pages answer their questions.

A useful SEO strategy connects three things: the needs of your audience, the quality of your website and the information you publish. The goal is relevant organic visibility that supports your business, not simply more visits to any page.

Which SEO services does your website need?

A newly launched website, a small service business and an established online store rarely need the same starting point. These are the main areas we can review and scope for your project.

01
SEO audit

Website SEO audits: find the issues and prioritize the work.

Before changing pages or publishing more content, it helps to know what is holding the website back. An SEO audit reviews the site as a connected system: what can be crawled, which pages are indexed, how content is organized and where useful journeys break down. We turn the findings into a prioritized action plan, so the next step is clear rather than another long list of unexplained errors.

What we work on

  • Crawl, indexing and website structure review
  • Content, internal links and search appearance checks
  • Prioritized findings with recommended next steps
02
Keyword research

SEO keyword research: understand what your customers need.

The most useful keyword is not always the one with the highest search volume. Keyword research connects your services or products to the language people use and the intent behind each search. We group commercial and long-tail queries by search intent, review competitor pages in the search results and map each topic to an existing or planned page. That gives each important page a clear purpose without repeating the same keywords everywhere.

What we work on

  • Search intent and competitor page review
  • Keyword mapping for service, product and content pages
  • Content priorities based on relevance and opportunity
03
On-page SEO & content

On-page SEO services: make each page a useful answer.

On-page SEO brings the content and structure of a page into focus. A clear title, useful headings, descriptive links and a relevant answer help people understand what you offer. We review copy, metadata, image descriptions and internal linking together, then recommend changes that make the page more useful. SEO content should explain the subject well; adding more repetitions of a phrase is not a substitute for a better answer.

What we work on

  • Page titles, meta descriptions and heading hierarchy
  • Content relevance, image descriptions and image delivery checks
  • Internal links between related pages
04
Technical SEO

Technical SEO services: help search engines reach your pages.

Good content still needs a working technical foundation. Technical SEO looks at the signals and site behavior that affect discovery, crawling and indexing. We review redirects, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, robots directives and mobile behavior, alongside performance and Core Web Vitals. Where implementation is included, our website and software development services can address agreed issues rather than leaving recommendations disconnected from the code.

What we work on

  • Crawlability, canonicalization and redirect checks
  • Mobile performance and Core Web Vitals review
  • Structured data and implementation validation
05
Off-page SEO

What does the rest of the web say about your business?

Off-page SEO considers relevant signals beyond your own website, including links and business mentions. We can review your backlink profile, identify relevant outreach opportunities and plan useful resources that people may choose to reference. The focus is on relevance and editorial value. We do not build a strategy around buying ranking links, automated link schemes or promises of a particular authority score.

What we work on

  • Backlink profile and competitor reference review
  • Relevant outreach and content promotion opportunities
  • Consistent business information across suitable listings
06
Local SEO

Local SEO services: help nearby customers find your business.

Local SEO helps a business communicate what it does and where it serves customers. For eligible businesses, that can include reviewing a Google Business Profile, accurate contact information and useful location-specific website pages. We consider local search terms and the questions customers ask before contacting you. The aim is to make the business easier to understand, without creating near-identical pages for places you do not genuinely serve.

What we work on

  • Google Business Profile review and optimization where eligible
  • Business details, service areas and local page content
  • Local keyword research and relevant listing consistency
07
Ecommerce SEO

Can shoppers find the right category and the right product?

An online store needs more than optimized product titles. Ecommerce SEO considers the relationship between categories, products, filters and changing inventory. We review navigation, product information, duplicate URLs and internal links, then prioritize the pages that help shoppers compare and choose. This can be scoped for WooCommerce or another store platform, with recommendations adapted to the controls and limitations of your setup.

What we work on

  • Category structure and product page optimization
  • Filter, duplicate URL and out-of-stock page handling
  • Relevant product markup and internal linking
08
SEO migration

Changing your website? What happens to the URLs people already find?

A redesign, domain change or platform move can alter the pages search engines know about. SEO migration planning connects the old site to the new one before launch. We can map existing URLs, review redirects, check important content and validate launch settings. After the move, agreed monitoring helps identify unexpected changes. Planning reduces avoidable mistakes; it does not make a migration risk-free.

What we work on

  • Old-to-new URL mapping and redirect planning
  • Pre-launch checks for content, links and indexing settings
  • Post-launch validation and issue review
09
International SEO

Do your pages make sense for every market you want to reach?

International SEO starts with the audience, language and offering in each target market. A translated page alone may not answer the same questions or match the same search intent. We can review regional content needs, site structure and language signals such as hreflang. The scope depends on the markets you actually serve and the resources available to maintain accurate, useful content for each audience.

What we work on

  • Language and regional search intent review
  • International URL structure and hreflang checks
  • Market-specific content and navigation recommendations
