Methodology

From messy data to operational revenue engines

Our engineering framework is built around data integrity, ethical AI deployment, and A/B-validated business results.

  1. 01

    Data Hygiene & Auditing

    We cleanse historical catalog, order, and behavioral event streams to establish a trusted training baseline.

  2. 02

    Model Design & Benchmarking

    We develop domain-specific ML models and test them against historical test data before production rollout.

  3. 03

    Canary A/B Deployment

    We route a slice of live traffic through canary splits to verify statistical significance and margin lift.

  4. 04

    MLOps & Drift Monitoring

    Automated retraining pipelines and drift monitors help keep recommendations fresh as customer trends evolve.

Key insights on custom LLM pipelines, workflow automation, enterprise data privacy, and production machine learning.

We identify high-leverage automation opportunities across your operations: intelligent retrieval (RAG), custom autonomous agents, automated document extraction, predictive analytics, and domain-specific conversational assistants.

Data privacy is paramount. We build private VPC-hosted AI pipelines, integrate enterprise APIs with zero-data-retention options where providers offer them, and deploy open-source models (Llama, Mistral) on your private cloud. Your data is never used to train public models.

RAG connects LLMs directly to your internal databases, documents, and product catalogs to provide verifiable answers grounded in your company's proprietary data in real time.

We architect scalable modern data stacks: automated ETL/ELT pipelines, vector databases (Pinecone, pgvector), data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery), and real-time event streaming pipelines using Python and dbt.
