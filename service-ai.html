Real-Time Personalization & Recommendation Engines

Deliver personalized product recommendations, personalized sorting, and tailored bundles tailored to individual buyer intent in real-time.

Semantic Search & Vector Product Discovery

Upgrade traditional keyword search to natural language vector embeddings that understand what shoppers actually mean, eliminating zero-result pages.

  • Hybrid lexical + dense vector embedding search engines
  • Automated typo tolerance, synonym expansion, and visual search
  • Facet generation based on seasonal trends and customer context

Deploy specialized AI agents with full catalog context that guide buyers, assist with sizing, and automate post-purchase inquiries.

Break down data silos between Shopify, your ERP, POS, and ad platforms into a unified, actionable customer single source of truth.

  • Reverse ETL data activation into Klaviyo, Meta, and Google Ads
