FAQ

Straight answers before you get in touch

Not answered here? Ask us directly, we reply within one business day.

What does PrimeLoft do?

PrimeLoft is a software development studio. We design and build websites, mobile apps and custom software, and we help them grow with SEO, cloud infrastructure and quality assurance. One senior team covers strategy, design, engineering and support.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

We are based in Pakistan and work remotely with clients across the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, Australia and the Gulf. We keep overlapping hours with your time zone and communicate in English via Slack, email and video calls.

How do you price projects?

Most projects are fixed-price with a defined scope and timeline, agreed before work starts. Ongoing work such as SEO, maintenance and managed hosting runs on month-to-month retainers with no long-term lock-in.

How quickly can we start?

You will hear back within one business day, receive a scoped proposal within 48 hours of a discovery call, and most projects kick off within one to two weeks.

Will we own everything you build?

Yes. Source code, designs, domains, hosting and third-party accounts are created in your name and handed over with documentation.

Can you take over a project from another agency or freelancer?

Yes. We start with a technical audit, stabilise what is urgent and give you a clear plan for the rest. Rescue projects are common for us.

Ready when you are

Tell us what you are building. We will tell you how we would build it.

A short brief is enough. You will hear from a senior partner within one business day, with questions and a suggested approach.
