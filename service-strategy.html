What is digital product strategy?

Digital product strategy connects a business goal with the needs of the people using a product. It defines the problem to solve, the audience, the priorities and how progress will be assessed.

For example, a strategy exercise can help decide whether to improve an existing checkout, launch a new customer portal or test a smaller prototype first. The result guides the scope and order of the work.
